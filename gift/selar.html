Open your free Selar creator account

A step-by-step guide to setting up your store, connecting your bank and publishing your first product, all from your phone.

⏱ About 10 minutes📱 Phone is enough₦0 to start

Before you start

  • An email address you check often (your Gmail is fine).
  • Your phone number.
  • Your bank account details, so Selar can pay you when you sell.
  • An idea for your first product. It can be simple: a short ebook, a checklist, a template or a class.
It's free. Selar doesn't charge you to open an account or list products. It only takes a small fee from each sale you make.

The steps

1

Go to Selar and sign up

Open selar.com in your phone browser (Chrome is fine) and tap the button to sign up or get started.

Fill in your name, email, phone number and a password, then choose your country.

2

Confirm your email

Selar sends you a confirmation email. Open it and tap the link inside. If you can't find it, check your Spam and Promotions tabs.

3

Set up your store

In your Selar dashboard, open your store settings and add:

  • Store name: your brand or your own name.
  • Store link (username): short and easy to say, e.g. selar.com/yourname. This is the link you'll share.
  • Profile photo and short bio: one or two lines on who you help and how.
  • Currency: Naira (NGN) if your buyers are in Nigeria.
4

Add your bank account for payouts

Go to the payout or bank settings and add the account Selar should pay your earnings into. Use an account in your own name.

Selar may ask you to verify your identity before your first withdrawal. Do it early so your money isn't held up when sales come in.
5

Create your first product

Go to Products and create a new product. Choose the type that fits (digital download, ebook, course, membership or event), then add:

  • Title: say the result, e.g. “30 Ready-Made Instagram Captions for Food Vendors”.
  • Price: start low for your first product so people can say yes easily.
  • Description: who it's for, what they get and what changes for them.
  • Cover image: a clean picture or a simple design made in Canva.
  • The file or content buyers receive after paying.
6

Publish and test your link

Publish the product and copy its link. Open the link in your browser to check that the page looks right.

7

Share it

Put your store link in your WhatsApp status, Instagram bio and Facebook posts. Every share is a chance for a sale, even while you sleep.

On Sunday's live training we go further: the sales page and the AI assistant that replies to your buyers 24/7. Have your Selar account ready so you can follow along live.

Ready?
