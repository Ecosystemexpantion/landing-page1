07Questions

Before you join.

Is the training really free?

Yes. Attending costs nothing and you won't be asked for card details. EEM26 also offers a paid, done-with-you setup called the Premium Tech Stack. We'll explain it at the end for anyone who wants extra help. It's completely optional.

What do I need?

A phone with enough data for a live video session. You don't need a laptop.

Do I need any experience?

No. We start from the beginning and show every step on screen. You don't need tech skills or an existing audience to follow along.

Where does the training happen?

Online. After you reserve your seat, we send your joining link by SMS and email before 8:00 PM on Sunday. If it doesn't arrive, check your Promotions and Spam folders.

Will there be a replay?

No. It's a live session and we don't post a replay, so plan to join on Sunday at 8:00 PM WAT.

Does it really work while I sleep?

The store, sales page and AI assistant run around the clock, so buyers can ask questions and pay at any hour, even when you're offline. Whether people buy still depends on your product and how you promote it, which we cover on Sunday.

How much will I earn?

We can't promise any amount, and you should be careful of anyone who does. The training shows you how the setup works. What you earn afterwards depends on the work you put in, the product you choose and your market.
